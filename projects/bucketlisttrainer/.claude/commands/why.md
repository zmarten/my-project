description: Explain the reasoning behind any session, block, or programming decision
---

Explain the reasoning for what the argument names (a week, a day, a block, or a
specific choice like "the weekly heavy single").

Structure the answer:

1. **The stimulus.** What physiological quality is targeted.
2. **The evidence.** What supports it, from `docs/methodology.md`. Name the concept and
   the researcher where you are confident. Do not invent citations or precision.
3. **The application.** Why it takes this specific form for this athlete, referencing
   his profile in `src/athlete.ts`.
4. **The honest limit.** What part of this is extrapolation or coaching judgement
   rather than established finding.

Section 4 is not optional. If everything in an answer sounds equally certain, the
answer is wrong.
