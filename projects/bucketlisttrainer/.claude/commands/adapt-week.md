---
description: Reshape a week around a real-life disruption without breaking periodization
---

The athlete needs a week changed. Arguments describe what happened.

1. Identify the week number and its block.
2. Read the block's `principles` and its invariant thresholds.
3. Propose the smallest change that absorbs the disruption. Cut in this order:
   accessory work, secondary sessions, then aerobic volume down to the block floor.
4. State explicitly which sessions are protected and why. In Block 1 that is Monday
   and Thursday, because two lower-body sessions per week is what preserves lean mass
   through the deficit.
5. Run `npm run check` and confirm zero errors.
6. If the disruption is recurring rather than one-off (a pattern of missed Fridays,
   an injury, a schedule change), say so and propose a structural fix to the block
   template instead of patching one week.

Never silently drop a session. If something has to go, name it and name the cost.
