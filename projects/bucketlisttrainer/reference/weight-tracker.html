Protocol

Same conditions every morning or the numbers mean nothing: after waking, after the bathroom, before food or water, minimal clothing. Miss a day and leave it blank rather than guessing. Five readings is enough for an honest weekly average.

Never act on a single day. Sodium, glycogen, and a big dinner move the scale 3 to 5 lbs with no change in body composition. The weekly average is the signal and everything else is noise.

Losing too fast is also a problem

The target arc runs about 0.86% of bodyweight per week, which is at the aggressive end of what preserves lean mass. Coming in consistently under target is not a bonus. It means you are probably shedding muscle you spent this block trying to keep.

If you are more than 3 lbs under target for two weeks running, eat more. Add roughly 200 calories a day and hold. The bar and ring muscle-ups get harder, not easier, if the weight comes off your back and shoulders.

The GLP-1 makes this a live risk rather than a theoretical one, because appetite suppression makes it easy to undershoot both calories and protein without noticing. Protein stays at 210 g regardless of what the scale is doing.

Strength checkpoints, weeks 6, 10 and 13

The scale is only half the measurement. At each deload and at the week 13 retest, compare your working loads against three weeks earlier:

  • Weight on track and lifts holding. The block is working. Change nothing.
  • Weight on track but lifts sliding. The cut is costing lean mass. Slow the rate to about 1.5 lb per week and check that protein is actually landing at 210 g.
  • Weight stalled but lifts climbing. Usually fine, especially early. Recomposition is real at your training age coming off a layoff. Hold for two more weeks before changing anything.
  • Weight stalled and lifts flat for three weeks. The deficit has closed as your TDEE fell. Drop about 150 calories, not 500.

Weighted pull-up belt load is the most sensitive of these, because it moves with both terms. If your belt weight is climbing week over week, the cut is doing exactly what it is supposed to.
